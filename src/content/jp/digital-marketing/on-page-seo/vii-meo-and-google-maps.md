MEO & Google Maps
**MEO** (Map Engine Optimization, マップエンジン最適化) is local visibility in **map packs** and **Google Maps** — not the same as ranking a website in blue links. In Japan the term is standard; elsewhere people say **local SEO**. Same work: a complete, accurate business profile plus matching on-page NAP.

A sitemap in GSC/Bing does **not** replace MEO. Maps results use the profile, reviews, proximity, and categories. See [Sitemaps — Google & Bing](vi-sitemaps-google-and-bing.md) for crawl files, and this note for storefronts.

## 1. SEO vs MEO

| | **SEO** | **MEO** |
|--|---------|---------|
| **Surface** | Web results | Maps, local pack, “near me” |
| **Primary asset** | Pages + links | [Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com) (GBP) |
| **Who it helps** | Any site | Businesses people visit, call, or book nearby |
| **Core signals** | Content, links, technical health | Relevance, distance, prominence (reviews, citations) |

Do both when you have a physical location or a service area. Skip MEO if you have no place customers can find on a map.

## 2. Claim and complete the profile

1. Open [Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com) and **claim** the listing (postcard, phone, or email).
2. Set the **primary category** to what people search (e.g. 整体, dentist) — extras are secondary only.
3. Fill **name, address, phone (NAP)**, hours, website, service area, and attributes (wheelchair, Wi‑Fi).
4. Add real **photos** (storefront, interior, team, product) — not stock-only.
5. Turn on **messaging** and keep **hours** current (holidays included).

| Field | Rule |
|-------|------|
| **Name** | Legal/public name — do not stuff keywords into the title |
| **Address** | Same as the door and the website footer |
| **Phone** | One local number you actually answer |
| **Website** | Canonical HTTPS URL of the location or service-area page |

**NAP consistency:** the same name, address, and phone on the site, GBP, and major directories. Mismatches split signals.

## 3. On-page support

| Page | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Location / contact** | Embedded map, NAP, hours, parking, `LocalBusiness` schema — [Internal linking & schema](iv-internal-linking-and-schema.md) |
| **Service + city pages** | One intent per URL — [Keywords & search intent](iii-keywords-and-search-intent.md) |
| **Local pack queries** | “near me” and city + category — match titles and H1s |

Do not create doorway pages for every neighborhood if you have one shop. Thin city clones get ignored.

## 4. Reviews and posts

| Practice | Why |
|----------|-----|
| Ask happy customers **after** a visit | Fresh reviews are a prominence signal |
| Reply to every review (good and bad) | Shows the listing is active |
| Never buy or gate reviews | Violates Google policy; risk of suspension |
| Weekly **GBP posts** / offers | Freshness; not a ranking silver bullet |

Photo and Q&A on the listing help relevance. Keep answers factual.

## 5. Bing and other maps

Google Maps is the main MEO surface in Japan. Still list the same NAP on:

| Directory | Notes |
|-----------|--------|
| **[Bing Places](https://www.bingplaces.com)** | Feeds Bing local; import from Google when possible |
| **Apple Business Connect** | Maps on iPhone |
| **Industry directories** | Hot Pepper, Tabelog, medical portals — only if they match the real NAP |

One accurate citation set beats twenty conflicting ones.

## 6. Measure

| Signal | Where |
|--------|--------|
| Discovery searches vs conversion (calls, routes, website) | GBP **Insights** / Performance |
| “Near me” and map-pack queries | [Search Console](../analytics/ii-search-console-and-rank-tracking.md) — filter device and query |
| Calls from the listing | Call history in GBP (if enabled) |

A website ranking #1 does not mean you win the **map pack**. Track both.

## 7. Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Keyword-stuffed business name | Use the real shop name |
| Service-area business with a fake pin | Hide address; set a service area |
| Closed hours / old menu photos | Update the same week they change |
| Duplicate listings | Merge or mark the extras closed |
| Treating MEO as “set once” | Hours, photos, and reviews are ongoing |
