AI SEO
**AI SEO** is making your pages useful enough that **answer engines** quote or cite them — Google **AI Overviews** / AI Mode, Bing Copilot, ChatGPT (with search), Perplexity, Gemini. Closely related labels: **AEO** (answer engine optimization) and **GEO** (generative engine optimization). The tactics overlap; the names are marketing.

This is **not** a replacement for crawl, index, and classic on-page. Models that search the web still need a fetchable URL. Do [Technical checklist](v-technical-checklist.md) and [Sitemaps — Google & Bing](vi-sitemaps-google-and-bing.md) first. Local businesses still need [MEO](vii-meo-and-google-maps.md).

## 1. SEO vs MEO vs AI SEO

| | **SEO** | **MEO** | **AI SEO** |
|--|---------|---------|------------|
| **Surface** | Blue links | Maps / local pack | AI answers and citations |
| **User action** | Click a result | Call, route, visit | Read a summary; maybe click a source |
| **Primary asset** | Pages + links | Google Business Profile | Pages that engines **trust enough to cite** |
| **Win looks like** | Rank + CTR | Map pack + calls | Mention / footnote / “source” chip |

Zero-click answers can **cut** organic CTR even when you are the source. Track citations and branded search, not only ranking position.

## 2. What engines actually use

Most consumer AI answers that talk about the live web **retrieve** pages (search index or browsing), then summarize. They do not “train on your blog overnight.”

| Signal | Why it still matters |
|--------|----------------------|
| **Indexable HTML** | JS-only text is easy to miss — same as classic crawl |
| **Clear entity** | Who you are, what the page is about, last updated |
| **Original facts** | Unique data, methods, or first-hand experience get cited more than rewrites |
| **E-E-A-T** | Experience, expertise, author, about — especially YMYL |
| **Inbound mentions** | Other sites naming you help the model pick a source |

Schema ([Internal linking & schema](iv-internal-linking-and-schema.md)) helps machines parse FAQ, HowTo, Product, and Article. It does not guarantee an AI Overview.

## 3. Page patterns that get cited

| Pattern | How |
|---------|-----|
| **Answer first** | One-sentence definition or number under the H2 that matches the query |
| **Question headings** | H2/H3 as the user would ask — then depth, not a keyword dump |
| **Tables and lists** | Easy for models to lift without inventing numbers |
| **Dated claims** | “As of 2026…” plus a source — stale pages lose citations |
| **Author + bio** | Real person, relevant credentials, same name sitewide |
| **Canonical URL** | One preferred page per topic — [Keywords & search intent](iii-keywords-and-search-intent.md) |

Write for a human who wants the answer in 10 seconds, then proof. That is also what retrieval + summarization prefers.

## 4. Surfaces to check

| Surface | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| **Google AI Overviews** | Search target queries in an incognito window; note if you appear as a source |
| **GSC** | Performance / AI-related filters when available; still use queries + pages |
| **Bing Copilot** | Same queries; Bing already has your sitemap if you submitted it |
| **Perplexity / ChatGPT search** | Ask the live question; see which URLs are footnoted |

You cannot “submit a sitemap to ChatGPT.” Brand + crawlable pages + citations on the open web are the input.

Optional and **unofficial**: a `/llms.txt` (or similar) that points models at preferred docs. Treat it as an experiment, not a ranking factor.

## 5. What not to do

| Mistake | Why it fails |
|---------|----------------|
| Mass AI-generated thin pages | Duplicate, low experience — skipped by index and by models |
| Hidden text / “LLM prompt” blocks | Spam risk; users and quality raters see it |
| Buying fake “AI citations” | Same class of problem as buying links |
| Ignoring classic SEO | If Google cannot index you, AI Overviews will not cite you |
| Optimizing only for one chatbot | Surfaces change; the durable work is clear, sourced pages |

## 6. Measure

| Signal | Use |
|--------|-----|
| Classic GSC clicks / impressions | Still the baseline |
| AI Overview / AI Mode appearance | Manual checks + GSC when Google exposes it |
| Branded query volume | People searching you after seeing a citation |
| Referral from `chat.openai.com`, Perplexity, Copilot | Sparse but real |

Do not expect a stable “AI rank” metric yet. Re-check a **short query list** monthly, same as a rank tracker sample.
