信義礼智仁 — the five constants
Five kanji shared by **儒教** and later **武士道** lists. Classical **五常** writes them **仁義礼智信**. This page uses **信義礼智仁** as the study order, then shows each character with readings and compounds.

Previous: [Overview](i-overview.md) · Next: [Bushido & samurai](iii-bushido-and-samurai.md)

## The five at a glance

| Kanji | English | On | Kun (common) | Core sense in ethics |
|-------|---------|----|--------------|----------------------|
| 信 | trust, faith | しん | まこと | Keep your word; be reliable |
| 義 | righteousness | ぎ | — | Do what is right, not what is easy |
| 礼 | courtesy, ritual | れい | — | Respect made visible; proper form |
| 智 | wisdom | ち | — | See clearly; judge well |
| 仁 | benevolence | じん / に | — | Treat people as human; compassion |

Read the string aloud: **しん・ぎ・れい・ち・じん**.

Classical 五常 order (仁義礼智信): **じん・ぎ・れい・ち・しん** — same characters.

## 信 — trust

Keeping faith with others. In modern Japanese this kanji is everywhere: 信用, 信頼, 自信, and also IT 信号 / 通信.

| Word | English | Reading |
|------|---------|---------|
| 信 | trust, faith | しん |
| 信用 | credit, trust (reputation) | しんよう |
| 信頼 | reliance, trust (relationship) | しんらい |
| 信念 | conviction | しんねん |
| 信条 | creed, principle | しんじょう |
| 自信 | self-confidence | じしん |
| 確信 | conviction that something is true | かくしん |
| 失信 | loss of trust | しっしん |
| 背信 | betrayal of trust | はいしん |
| 信義 | good faith, fidelity | しんぎ |

**Contrast:** 誠 (まこと) is inner sincerity. 信 is whether others can **count on** you.

## 義 — righteousness

The duty to act justly. Also the ぎ in 義務, 正義, 定義, and 主義.

| Word | English | Reading |
|------|---------|---------|
| 義 | righteousness, justice | ぎ |
| 正義 | justice | せいぎ |
| 義務 | duty, obligation | ぎむ |
| 義理 | social duty; “I owe this” | ぎり |
| 義侠 | chivalry, helping the weak | ぎきょう |
| 仁義 | humanity and justice; underworld “honor” | じんぎ |
| 主義 | -ism, principle | しゅぎ |
| 定義 | definition | ていぎ |
| 名義 | name (in whose name) | めいぎ |
| 大義 | a just cause | たいぎ |

**Note:** 義理 is living Japanese — favors, wedding gifts, attending a funeral. It is heavier than English “duty.”

## 礼 — courtesy

Form that shows respect. Office Japanese still runs on 礼: 失礼します, 礼儀, 礼を言う.

| Word | English | Reading |
|------|---------|---------|
| 礼 | courtesy, thanks, bow | れい |
| 礼儀 | manners, etiquette | れいぎ |
| 礼節 | decorum | れいせつ |
| 礼儀作法 | etiquette, protocol | れいぎさほう |
| 失礼 | rudeness; “excuse me” | しつれい |
| 敬礼 | salute | けいれい |
| 一礼 | a slight bow | いちれい |
| 謝礼 | honorarium, thanks-money | しゃれい |
| 婚礼 | wedding ceremony | こんれい |
| 無礼 | rude, insolent | ぶれい |

In Confucianism, 礼 is not only politeness — it is the **ritual order** that keeps relationships from collapsing into force.

## 智 — wisdom

The virtue of clear judgment. Everyday “know / information” usually uses **知**, not 智.

| Word | English | Reading |
|------|---------|---------|
| 智 | wisdom (virtue) | ち |
| 知恵 | wisdom, know-how | ちえ |
| 智慧 | wisdom (Buddhist / elevated) | ちえ |
| 智力 | intellect | ちりょく |
| 智略 | resourcefulness | ちりゃく |
| 智謀 | strategy, cunning | ちぼう |
| 智者 | a wise person | ちしゃ |
| 理智 | reason, intellect | りち |
| 才智 | talent and wit | さいち |
| 智仁勇 | wisdom, benevolence, courage | ちじんゆう |

| 知 (everyday) | English | Reading |
|---------------|---------|---------|
| 知る | to know | しる |
| 知識 | knowledge | ちしき |
| 知能 | intelligence | ちのう |
| 通知 | notice | つうち |
| 承知 | acknowledgment | しょうち |

**三徳** (さんとく): **智・仁・勇** — wisdom, benevolence, courage. A shorter Confucian set that Bushido also likes.

## 仁 — benevolence

The central Confucian virtue: humanity, kindness that recognizes the other person as a person.

| Word | English | Reading |
|------|---------|---------|
| 仁 | benevolence, humanity | じん |
| 仁愛 | charity, humane love | じんあい |
| 仁者 | a humane person | じんしゃ |
| 仁義 | humanity and justice | じんぎ |
| 仁侠 | chivalrous spirit | にんきょう |
| 仁政 | humane government | じんせい |
| 仁術 | the art of healing (medicine as 仁) | じんじゅつ |
| 不仁 | inhuman, cruel | ふじん |

Reading split: じん in 仁義 / 仁愛; にん in 仁侠 (にんきょう).

## Other Confucian virtue sets

These reuse 信義礼智仁 and add a few more characters. Full compounds are on [Compounds & sayings](iv-compounds-and-sayings.md).

| Set | Japanese | Members |
|-----|----------|---------|
| **五常** | ごじょう | 仁義礼智信 |
| **三徳** | さんとく | 智仁勇 |
| **五倫** | ごりん | Five relationships (ruler–subject, parent–child, husband–wife, elder–younger, friends) |
| **八徳** | はっとく | 孝悌忠信礼義廉恥 |

| Kanji | English | On | Why it appears |
|-------|---------|----|----------------|
| 孝 | filial piety | こう | Duty to parents |
| 悌 | respect for elders / siblings | てい | Harmony in the household |
| 忠 | loyalty | ちゅう | Duty to lord or office |
| 廉 | integrity, honesty | れん | Clean hands |
| 恥 | shame | ち / はじ | Knowing when you have fallen short |

## Study drill

Cover the English column and say the reading.

1. 信義 → しんぎ
2. 仁義 → じんぎ
3. 礼儀 → れいぎ
4. 知恵 → ちえ
5. 仁愛 → じんあい
6. 五常 → ごじょう
7. 智仁勇 → ちじんゆう
