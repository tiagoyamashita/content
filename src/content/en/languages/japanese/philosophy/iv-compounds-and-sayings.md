Compounds & sayings — living remnants
Two-kanji words, school names, and set phrases built from [信義礼智仁](ii-five-constants.md) and [Bushido & samurai](iii-bushido-and-samurai.md). Many are still used in companies, schools, and ordinary speech.

Previous: [Bushido & samurai](iii-bushido-and-samurai.md) · Overview: [Overview](i-overview.md)

## How to use this page

1. Learn the **compound** as a unit (reading + one English gloss).
2. Split it only when you forget: `忠孝` → loyalty + filial piety.
3. Mark which words you already say at work (信用, 義務, 礼儀) vs period / motto words (仁政, 王道, 葉隠).

**義理 vs 人情.** 義理 is what you **owe** the group. 人情 is what you **feel**. Japanese stories often put them in conflict; 義理人情 names that pair.
