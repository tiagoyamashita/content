Philosophy — 信義礼智仁 and related words
Kanji and compounds from **儒教** (Confucianism), **武士道** (Bushido), and **侍** (samurai) thought. These characters still appear in mottos, school names, martial-arts dojos, literature, and everyday words such as 信用 and 礼儀.

This track is **language study**, not a history essay. Learn the **character**, then the **compound**, then the **set phrase**.

## How the traditions overlap

| Tradition | Japanese | What it contributes here |
|-----------|----------|--------------------------|
| **Confucianism** | 儒教 (じゅきょう) | The five constants **仁義礼智信** — same five kanji as **信義礼智仁** |
| **Bushido** | 武士道 (ぶしどう) | A later, often Meiji-era, framing of warrior ethics; borrows Confucian virtues and adds 勇, 誠, 名誉, 忠義 |
| **Samurai** | 侍 / 武士 | Social role and older words: 士, 主君, 家臣, 覚悟, 名誉, 恥 |

**Order of the five.** Classical **五常** (ごじょう) is **仁・義・礼・智・信**. The string **信義礼智仁** uses the same five characters in a common Japanese listing. Study the characters; do not treat one order as the only “correct” motto.

**Bushido as a word.** Lived Edo-period ethics varied by domain and drew heavily on **朱子学**. The tidy list of seven virtues is best known from Nitobe Inazō’s *Bushido* (1900). Use it as a **vocabulary map**, not as a claim that every historical samurai recited the same code.

## How to use this track

1. Start with [信義礼智仁](ii-five-constants.md) — five characters, on-yomi first.
2. Add the extra Bushido kanji on [Bushido & samurai](iii-bushido-and-samurai.md) (勇, 誠, 忠, 孝, 名誉).
3. Learn **two-kanji words** and **set phrases** on [Compounds & sayings](iv-compounds-and-sayings.md).
4. When a compound is new, **split it** (`信義` → trust + righteousness).

**Rules of thumb**

- In ethical compounds, **on-yomi** dominates (`仁義` → じんぎ, `忠義` → ちゅうぎ).
- **智** is the virtue form; everyday “know” is usually **知** (`知識`, `通知`).
- **信** (trust / keeping one’s word) and **誠** (sincerity / makoto) overlap in English but are different kanji.
