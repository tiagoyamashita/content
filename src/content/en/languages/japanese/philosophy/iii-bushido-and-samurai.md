Bushido & samurai — warrior vocabulary
Words for **武士道**, **侍**, and the honor cluster around them. Pair with [信義礼智仁](ii-five-constants.md) for the five shared virtues, then [Compounds & sayings](iv-compounds-and-sayings.md) for set phrases.

**侍 vs 武士.** 侍 is the everyday / popular word. 武士 is the class and the compounds (武士道, 武士気質). 士 is the bound form in 紳士, 兵士, 弁護士 — “a person of a role.”

## Nitobe’s seven virtues

A common modern list (Nitobe Inazō). Four overlap 五常; three are extra.

| Virtue | Kanji | Reading | English | Overlap |
|--------|-------|---------|---------|---------|
| Rectitude | 義 | ぎ | do the right thing | 五常 |
| Courage | 勇 | ゆう | act despite fear | extra |
| Benevolence | 仁 | じん | compassion | 五常 |
| Respect | 礼 | れい | courtesy | 五常 |
| Honesty | 誠 | まこと / せい | sincerity | extra (near 信) |
| Honor | 名誉 | めいよ | name, reputation | extra |
| Loyalty | 忠義 | ちゅうぎ | loyal duty | extra (忠 + 義) |

## Honor, shame, resolve

The moral pressure around 武士 is often **name** and **shame**, not only “rules.”

| Word | English | Reading |
|------|---------|---------|
| 名誉 | honor | めいよ |
| 面目 | face; standing | めんぼく |
| 面子 | face (social standing) | めんつ |
| 恥 | shame | はじ |
| 恥を知る | to have a sense of shame | はじをしる |
| 廉恥 | sense of honor and shame | れんち |
| 覚悟 | resolve; readiness to accept the cost | かくご |
| 覚悟を決める | to steel oneself | かくごをきめる |
| 死生観 | view of life and death | しせいかん |
| 切腹 | ritual suicide | せっぷく |
| 殉死 | following one’s lord in death | じゅんし |
| 仇 | foe; vengeance target | あだ / かたき |
| 仇討ち | vendetta | あだうち |
| 仇討 | revenge (written form) | あだうち |
| 武士の情け | a warrior’s mercy | ぶしのなさけ |
| 武士に二言はない | a warrior does not take back his word | ぶしににごんはない |

**覚悟** is still ordinary Japanese: “I’m prepared for this” (a hard talk, a resignation, a launch). It is not only a period-drama word.
